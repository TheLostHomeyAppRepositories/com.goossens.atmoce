See your Atmoce system in Homey Energy and let Homey steer your battery. Homey talks directly to the Atmoce gateway over your home network, without the cloud: solar production, battery level and power, and what you take from and return to the grid update every few seconds. Let Homey set the battery's charge or discharge power, or start a forced charge or discharge from a Flow, for example when electricity is cheap.

Works with the Atmoce M-Combiner MC100 and MC100-T and the M-Gateway MG100, with every Atmoce battery connected to them. Turn on Modbus-TCP in the Atmozen app under Settings → 3rd Party System before adding your devices. The MC100L (Lite) has no local connection and is not supported.

Bring your Atmoce solar and battery system into Homey. Solar production, battery level and every watt you take from or send to the grid appear live in Homey Energy, read straight from the Atmoce gateway on your home network, without the cloud. The Energy flow widget shows on your dashboard how the sun charges your battery and powers your home, and when the system reports a fault, a battery needs attention or the power goes out, a clear message lands on your timeline.

Then put your energy to work with Flows. Charge the battery when electricity is cheap and save it for the evening peak, cap export or solar production when prices turn negative, and act the moment the battery reaches a level or the panels start producing. Works with the M-Combiner MC100 and MC100-T and the M-Gateway MG100 with any Atmoce battery: switch on Modbus-TCP in the Atmozen app under Settings, 3rd Party System, and Homey finds your gateway by itself. To let Homey charge the battery from the grid, also turn on Grid recharging under Battery working mode. The MC100L Lite has no local connection and is not supported.
